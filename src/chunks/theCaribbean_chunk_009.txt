UNICEF has delivered 29 new water tanks (236 m³ capacity) and 69,000 water purification tablet
packages, benefiting up to 16,000 people daily. Three additional water purification plants for Granma will
provide safe drinking water for 21,000 people.
UNDP is providing 6,000 metal roofing sheets, 3,900 tarpaulins, toolkits, and generators to support
recovery in Granma and Guantánamo.
UNFPA has supplied sexual and reproductive health kits, contraceptives, and clinical management
materials for gender-based violence cases.
PAHO/WHO is delivering medical equipment, fumigation kits, and generators to reinforce health services
and vector control in affected provinces.
WFP has reached 140,000 people with food distributions in protection centers and communal kitchens in
Granma, Santiago de Cuba, Guantánamo, and Holguín.
