RESPONSE
REGIONAL
The United Nations and partners are supporting Government-led response efforts in the three countries.
In Jamaica, Rapid Needs Assessment Teams—including those from the United States (DART), the United Kingdom
(FCDO), CDEMA regional teams, and several international NGOs—are working alongside national authorities to assess
needs and coordinate the response. OCHA is providing support for coordination and information management, helping to
ensure consistent information flows among partners and effective planning across sectors.
• The Government of Jamaica, supported by humanitarian and development partners, continues to lead the national
response, prioritizing access restoration, life-saving assistance, and early recovery. Prime Minister Andrew Holness
announced recovery measures including targeted rebuilding support by parish, a six-month mortgage moratorium for
