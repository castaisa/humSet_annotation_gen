A private-sector liaison officer is deloying from the OCHA/Connecting Business Initiative to facilitate connections
between international, regional and national private-sector partners, and to support the strengthening of coordination
with the Private Sector Organisation of Jamaica (PSOJ), the Jamaica Chamber of Commerce and the Jamaica
Manufacturers' Association (JMA).
• The Caribbean Catastrophe Risk Insurance Facility (CCRIF SPC) has disbursed US$92 million to the Government
under its parametric insurance policies, providing critical liquidity for early response and recovery efforts.
• The Government of Cuba continues to lead emergency operations, focusing on reaching isolated communities,
restoring essential services, and providing food, water, and health assistance to the most affected populations. Water
and sanitation restoration remains a top priority, with pumping units repaired and deployed to ensure safe water
access. Progress has been made in restoring electricity and water networks—up to 80 per cent service restoration in
some provinces—though significant gaps remain in Granma and Santiago de Cuba.
