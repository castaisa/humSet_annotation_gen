• The United Nations, supported by a UN Disaster Assessment and Coordination (UNDAC) team of 16 members, is
coordinating relief efforts with the Government of Jamaica and partners. More than 60 organizations, including
authorities, donors, NGOs, UN agencies, and the private sector, have joined the OCHA-led coordination system to
register assistance, offers, and deliveries.
• To strengthen medical capacity, Samaritan’s Purse has deployed an emergency field hospital at Black River High
School in St. Elizabeth, replacing the destroyed Black River Hospital. The facility is staffed by 60 personnel and includes
a pharmacy, laboratory, and operating room, serving as the main medical center for the parish until permanent
infrastructure is restored.
• The Pan-American Health Organization (PAHO) has delivered over 5.5 tons of medical supplies and trauma kits and

health to support care continuity and disease surveillance. On 4 November, they launched a Donor Alert seeking
