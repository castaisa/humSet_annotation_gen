• On 5 November, the United Nations System in Cuba, together with the Government, has launched a Plan of Action to
support the national response to the devastating impact of Hurricane Melissa. The plan aims to assist one million
people among the 2.2 million in need across the eastern provinces of Granma, Santiago de Cuba, Holguín, and
Guantánamo, outlining a coordinated response by UN agencies in support of the Government and the Civil Defense
System. It calls for US$74 million, of which US$4 million was released ahead of the hurricane from the Central
Emergency Response Fund (CERF) under its Anticipatory Action programme, and another US$7 million has been
contributed from UN agencies’ own resources—leaving a funding gap of about US$64 million to meet urgent needs.
The response focuses on health, water and sanitation, shelter, education, and early recovery, with special attention to
the needs of women, children, and other vulnerable groups.
