• Hurricane Melissa devastated Jamaica, striking key economic and population centres and affecting close to 1.6
million people. The western and southern parishes remain hardest hit, littered with debris, blocked access routes, and
interrupted essential services. According to UNDP, over 4.8 million tonnes of debris—about 480,000 truckloads—clog
roads, schools, and public spaces.
• Access and logistics constraints continue to hamper the scale-up of operations, particularly in western Jamaica. At
least 30 communities remain cut off by damaged or obstructed roads, while unstable slopes continue to trigger
landslides and flash floods.
• Relief agencies report urgent needs for safe water, fuel for health facilities, food, shelter materials, and psychosocial
support. Health services remain overstretched, with many facilities still relying on generators and temporary supply
networks.
