• The World Food Programme (WFP) and partners are working to stabilize supply chains through the Logistics Control
Tower, reopening key corridors and exploring alternate delivery routes via the Dominican Republic. The Government
has announced the establishment of 22 humanitarian hubs, led by the Jamaica Defence Force (JDF), to coordinate
relief operations and improve delivery to hard-hit areas.
• Two international naval vessels have also arrived to reinforce logistics operations — a French ship carrying water-
treatment units and emergency shelter materials, and the Dutch naval support ship HNLMS Pelikaan with over 600 m³
of WASH, shelter, and medical supplies.
• On 4 November, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) launched a Flash Appeal of US$17.2 million to
support the response for the period November 2025 to April 2026. The appeal aims to assist 96,000 people—including
48,000 women and 14,000 persons with disabilities—across the four hardest-hit parishes of Westmoreland, Hanover,
Saint James, and Trelawny, where 468,000 people, or 92 per cent of the population, were severely affected.
